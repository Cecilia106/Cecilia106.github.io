About me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Finance and Risk Engineering at New York University. I joined NYU in Fall 2025 and am honored to be supervised by Prof. Xin Zhang. Previously, I earned an M.Sc. in Applied Mathematics at the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology Zurich (ETH Zurich, 2022–2025), where I was advised by Prof. Martin Schweizer. I received my Bachelor of Science from The University of Hong Kong (2018–2022).

I am interested in optimal transport and its applications in robust finance and machine learning.
